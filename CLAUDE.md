## How it fits together

- `rules/sim.js` owns the match: `stepSim(world, intent, dt)` takes one intent per frame (move, facing, shot, pass, knock, drag, call, strike, head) and returns events (`windup`, `kick`, `pass`, `launched`, `dribble`, `received`, `goal`, `saved`, `post`, `out`, `tackled` and so on). The page never moves the ball or players itself. The sim also owns every player's run cycle (`p.stride`); the page draws the legs from it.
- `rules/match.js` turns events into points and banners and runs the 2-minute clock.
- `game/main.js` reads controls, builds the intent (`game/intent.js`: head-aim shooting, pass ring holds, your head for headers), steps the sim, records it for replays, then poses models and HUD.
- All feel numbers live in `rules/tuning.js`.

## Rules

- Plain ES modules, no bundler, no runtime npm packages. three.js is pinned to **r149** from jsDelivr via the import map.
- Game rules stay pure (no three, no DOM) so they're testable. Every new rules module gets a test file.
- Keep files under about 400 lines.
- `npm run check` must pass before committing. Run `npm run smoke` when a page changes.
- PS1 look: use `ps1()` from `game/ps1.js` for every material (vertex snap, affine textures, dither). Its strength is tunable in `PS1` (snap grid, color levels, dither amount); Kenny asked for it softer than the real PS1. Textures are painted in code at small sizes with nearest filtering.
- Kits are colors only: no real crests, sponsors, club or player names.

## Quest 3 budgets

72 fps minimum. Each player is one draw call (a SkinnedMesh). The headset renders at framebuffer scale 0.7.

## Decision log

- Name: Super Soccer Star '96. 90s logo and pixel-font UI.
- Comfort isn't a constraint (Kenny's call: it's just for him). Smooth turning is fine.
- Controls: sticky-ball dribbling (the ball stays yours; since Nov 2026 it's tapped along stride by stride, see "The boot and the ball"), head-aimed shooting, timing-ring passes (hold, release when the ring meets the circle).
- First mode: 3 v 3 plus keeper on a half pitch, 2-minute arcade score attack.
- Cups (Kenny's format): every match is a comeback scenario (start behind, short clock, reach the target). Opponents never attack in play; they can score on the counter when you lose the ball (`counterAttack` in `rules/match.js`). A defender winning the rebound off a parry, block or the woodwork (`isRebound` in `rules/sim.js`, within `REBOUND.window` with nobody kicking it since) is a clearance: CLEARED banner, no INTERCEPTED call, and a third of the counter chance (`REBOUND.counter`). Before Nov 2026 those counted as interceptions and caused ~80% of counter goals, so shooting on target was the riskiest thing you could do. The cup clock stays as it is (Kenny). World Cup '96 = 32 nations, groups of 4 with draws then knockouts. Champions Cup = 17 city clubs, 16 drawn per cup (yours always in), knockouts. 3 arcade continues per cup for knockout losses; going out in the groups is final.
- Teams (agreed list): 32 nations in `rules/teams.js`; 17 clubs by city name (London Red and London Blue both in, Istanbul added Nov 2026 in red/yellow halves, Marseille out). No club names or crests.
- Menus are in-world panels for both headset and browser. Picking is A/X or Enter, never a trigger (a held trigger would fire a shot at kick-off). Shoot and pass also ignore input until both triggers have been released after kick-off.
- Winning a cup ends with a physical trophy lift: grab with grips, raise above your head. The rig isn't pinned to the player during the ceremony (pinning cancelled real steps and left Kenny stuck, Nov 2026); the plinth stands 0.6 m away; the lift counts once a holding fist is 12 cm over your eyes; after 20 s A continues anyway.
- Team stars matter on the pitch (`rules/skill.js`, `tuneFor(yours, theirs)`): their stars scale pressing, tackling, interceptions and keeper; yours scale teammates' pace and passing and a little of your pace and shooting. Max ±10% between 1 and 5 stars (`SPREAD`; 15% compounded too hard, and 20% on pressing or keeper reach halved 5 v 5 win rates). Nov 2026 (Kenny: make stars felt, same odds): what you see without it deciding every attack moves ±20% (`SEEN`): how hard a shot their keeper holds, your teammates' pace, how often their passes to you are perfect (45% at 1 star, 95% at 5). To keep cup odds, the counter chance and starting deficit lean less on stars (`counterChance` 0.16 + 0.025 x their stars, deficit term 0.03 x star gap). Measured over 12 World Cups each with a scripted player: Saudi Arabia 1, Canada 3, Brazil 10 (was 1, 3, 12).
- World Cup team select is a world map: the stick moves to the nearest nation that way; European picks zoom to Europe.
- PS1 wobble is a setting (OFF / LOW / FULL, default LOW) on the settings screen. In the headset LOW is a light vertex wobble with no texture warp (warp swims as your head moves); FULL adds the warp back.
- Match length is a setting (SHORT 0.6x, NORMAL, LONG 1.5x, EPIC 2.5x): Score Attack's 2 minutes and every cup scenario's clock scale with it. Settings persist in localStorage.
- The keeper always faces the ball in a crouched ready stance and side-steps instead of running; he hugs the ball after a catch and eases into position (no twitching). Keeper animation lives in `game/keeperpose.js` (Oct 2026 redo, Kenny asked for better ones): the sim sets `gk.save { y, kind, id }` and `gk.dive` (-1/0/1; 0 is a standing save for shots within `KEEPER.standSave` of him); the page latches it and plays a load, a dive flat (low) or up and across (high) with hands leading, a catch gathered into the chest or a stiff-wristed parry, landing on his side and up through one knee. Standing saves scoop, chest or catch overhead. A split step on every strike, looser when the ball's far. `tools/lab.html?pose=dive&y=..&save=..&times=..` freezes moments side by side.
- Passes lob over defenders in the lane, otherwise go along the ground (`rules/pass.js` choosePass). The lane is a corridor that widens toward the receiver (`PASS.lobNear`/`lobFar` in tuning). A blue ring and a LOB tag show it before you pass.
- On a hat trick (your 3rd, 6th... goal): a rhythm dance mini-game (`rules/dance.js`, `game/celebration.js`) at 88 bpm, one move per beat. Teammates face you and mirror the moves.
- Right stick is only for turning. Skills are on buttons: A knock-on (standing still: flick-up), B drag-back, Y restart.
- Your own body in first person shows only boots and lower socks, plus low-poly hands on the controller grips (`game/hands.js`): open by default, pointing while you squeeze the grip, a fist on the trigger, sweatband in your kit. They follow the WebXR grip-space spec (origin mid-fist, -Z along the handle toward the thumb, palm toward -X on the right hand, +Y back to the wrist).
- Trophies are deliberately PS1: few sides, flat shading, dithered metal textures, engraved plates. While you grip a hand-hold, a posed hand on the cup replaces your controller hand; each hand takes its own side (left hand on the cup's left).
- Every club has its own ground (`game/grounds.js` recipes: stand tiers, corners, roof, lights, time of day, mowing, seat colors, extras like Liverpool's single big end, Dortmund's wall, Turin's track, Milan's towers; the two Milan clubs share a ground). Champions Cup alternates: home in R16 and SF, away in QF and the final. Nations and the World Cup use the national stadium.
- The crowd is ~10k instanced fan dots in both teams' colors; they bob all game, yours jump when your team scores, theirs when they score on the counter (vertex shader, one draw call).
- Skill moves (`rules/skills.js`, gestures in `rules/gestures.js`, setting SKILL MOVES OFF / GESTURE / GRIP + GESTURE, default GESTURE): step-over (right-hand sideways flick: fakes that way, you burst the other), roulette (left hand, any sharp flick or a quick circle: spin round him and on, shielded; it moved to the left hand because a right-hand circle's upward part read as a rainbow), nutmeg (right-hand jab forward-down: through the legs of a man square in front, he's stunned and you collect it beyond him) and rainbow flick (right-hand upward flick: over his head, run onto it). Gestures are sharp hand flicks relative to your head in your body's frame (1.9 m/s+), only on the ball with a defender within 5 m, not while charging a shot (right) or holding the pass ring (left), 0.6 s cooldown; in GRIP + GESTURE mode the right grip is the clutch for both hands; Kenny plays with his hands at his sides so no grip is needed by default. Browser keys 1-4. A presser falls for the same move twice in a row (SKILL.bite), then reads it; mixing moves keeps working. Style points per skill; a goal after 2+ skills in the attack is JOGA BONITA (+300 each). Each move has a training drill with a live presser.

- How players move (Nov 2026, Kenny: they "look stiff as hell"; drives both the PS1 and the PS2-look bodies). `animate()` in `game/actors.js` runs: `faceBody` → `poseLegs` (`game/gait.js`) → `strideLegs` (captured strides over the legs when he goes forward, `game/mocap.js`) and `leanHips` (the lean into a turn put back on the captured pelvis) → strikes and touches (`game/strike.js`) → `poseUpper` → `strideTrunk` (captured trunk and arms) → layers (`game/carriage.js`; `layerLean`'s forward part is switched off under a captured stride, which has its own) → `sway` (`game/sway.js`) → header / dance / celebrating (`game/poses.js`). The procedural gait is all there is for side-steps, backing off, standing, turning and the first-person boots.
  - Feet first: each foot follows a path over the ground (planted, rolling heel to toe on its own pivots, then carried) and the legs are bent to it; a planted foot moves back at the body's own speed in the direction of travel, so it grips forwards, sideways and backwards. The hips ride on the legs (never higher than both ankles can be reached). A foot in the air swings in line with its own hip (IK with a folded knee threw the knee in or out otherwise).
  - The cycle is the sim's (`p.stride`) whenever he runs or has the ball. A walk, a side-step and a braking step have their own quicker rate (`g.ph` drifts off `p.stride` and is pulled back as he runs): the sim's rate at walking pace means 1 m steps, which either slide or float.
  - The model's heading is eased after the sim's facing (which snaps to the velocity), tight when he is on the ball or striking. A defender moving under ~2.7 m/s within 13 m of the ball keeps his chest to it and backs off or side-steps (jockeying: the sim knows nothing of it). A teammate left standing with the ball gone behind him turns to it. Standing feet stay planted while the body turns and step round one at a time.
