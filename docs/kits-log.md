# Kits log

One row per team. How it was done: docs/kits.md. Era: 2003-04, give or take two years. Made 20 Nov 2026 by the main session, with three research helpers.

Honest summary of the era research: shirt, shorts and sock colors were confirmed for most European and South American nations and for most clubs' change kits. Trim, panel and number details could almost never be confirmed from text (kit sites are image-only or block automated reading), so `cut` and `hoops` are mostly design choices, spread evenly. For the 15 nations from URU to CAN other than SEN nothing could be confirmed at all: their change kits follow the template's fallback rules and want a look from Kenny. Also changed on his say: STAR·96 wordmark removed from all shirts; CRO home socks white; FRA home shorts kept white.
